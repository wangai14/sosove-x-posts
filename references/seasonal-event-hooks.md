# Seasonal Event Hooks

Use this reference to make SOSOVE X posts feel timely, relatable, and purchase-oriented.

## Goal

Each daily batch should connect a fashion opinion to a current Japan lifestyle moment. The best post makes the reader think: "This is exactly my problem this week."

## Current-Fact Rule

Verify current facts before using them. Browse or inspect a reliable source when mentioning:

- A specific holiday date.
- Weather, heat waves, rain, humidity, pollen, typhoons, or temperature.
- Trending events, celebrity/news hooks, sales periods, or platform trends.
- Product price, stock, discount, material, or shipping claims.

If not verified, write the hook generally: "rainy days", "humid mornings", "summer commute", "office AC", "vacation prep".

## Japan Seasonal Hooks

- Late winter: wardrobe refresh, cold mornings, heavy outerwear fatigue.
- March-April: graduation, entrance ceremony, new work season, cherry blossom outings, pollen.
- Late April-May: Golden Week, travel, early summer heat, light layering.
- June-July: rainy season, humidity, white clothes anxiety, office AC vs outdoor heat, sandals, summer commute.
- August: heat, Obon travel, vacation photos, sweat, dark outfit heaviness.
- September: late-summer fatigue, autumn transition, still-hot afternoons.
- October-November: layering, school events, autumn outings, knit and denim balance.
- December: year-end gatherings, holiday dinners, cold-weather polish, gift season, sale fatigue.

## High-Conversion Tension Points

- Safe black vs seasonally heavy.
- Loose comfort vs sloppy appearance.
- Body cover vs polished silhouette.
- Trendy outfit vs actually wearable daily clothes.
- Sweet skirt vs too youthful.
- White clothes anxiety vs clean summer look.
- Cheap clothes vs cheap-looking fabric and shape.
- Hot weather comfort vs looking put together.

## Post Formula

Use this structure for most posts:

1. Seasonal trigger: "humid morning", "rainy commute", "office AC", "summer black outfit".
2. Controversial observation: "safe but heavy", "comfortable but sloppy", "covering too much makes it look worse".
3. Reframe: what to choose instead.
4. Soft desire: clean, light, polished, easy, adult.
5. Soft CTA: mention SOSOVE only if it feels natural.

## CTA Direction

Prefer desire-led CTAs:

- For people who want summer clothes that feel light but still look polished.
- For adults who want easy clothes without looking careless.
- If you are tired of choosing only black because it feels safe.
- If you want "not too sweet" daily clothes.

Do not overuse direct CTA in every post. A batch of 5 should include 2-3 posts with no brand mention and 2-3 posts with a soft SOSOVE nudge.
